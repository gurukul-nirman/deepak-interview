# T09 · AI governance & AI regulation — quick refresher
**Study:** `04_governance_regulation/04_ai_governance.md` · **Test:** `START TOPIC T09`

## 60-second summary
- **AI governance ≠ MRM:** MRM validates models; AI governance decides whether/how a use case proceeds (privacy, security, IP, conduct, third-party, agents).
- Operating model: board-approved AI policy → AI council → 3LoD → **inventory** → **tiering** → impact assessment → lifecycle gates → monitoring/incidents → board KRIs.
- **EU AI Act:** credit scoring of natural persons = **high-risk** (fraud detection excluded); deployers: Art. 26 duties + **Art. 27 FRIA** before first use; Art. 86 explanations; obligations from **2 Dec 2027**; AI literacy since 2 Feb 2025; fines up to €35m/7%.
- **NIST AI RMF:** Govern · Map · Measure · Manage; AI 600-1 adds 12 GenAI risks. **ISO 42001:** certifiable AI management system (38 Annex A controls).
- **India FREE-AI (13 Aug 2025):** 7 Sutras, 26 recommendations, 6 pillars. **MAS AIRM:** proposed Nov 2025, still pending (Oct 2026). **AI Omnibus (Reg. 2026/1744)** in force 27 Jul 2026 — the Dec 2027 date is law. **SR 26-2:** GenAI/agentic out of scope.

## Quick Q → short answer
| Q | A |
|---|---|
| Tiering dimensions? | Decision impact, autonomy, customer exposure, data sensitivity, opacity/novelty, scale, regulatory class |
| AI inventory fields? | Use case, owner, AI type, vendor/version, data, decision impact, oversight, jurisdiction/class, tier, assessments, testing, controls, monitoring, review dates |
| Human-in vs on-the-loop? | Approves each decision vs supervises with ability to intervene |
| Who governs a GenAI chatbot under SR 26-2? | AI governance framework (not MRM guidance), with MRM-style evidence |
| FRIA? | Fundamental rights impact assessment — required of credit-scoring deployers before first use |
| FREE-AI key recs? | Board AI policy, AI inventories, incident reporting & audit, disclosures, graded liability, sandbox |
| Third-party AI controls? | Due diligence, contract rights (audit, change notice, data use), local testing, version pinning, exit plan |
| Agentic AI controls? | Least privilege, sandbox, limits, human approval for high impact, logs, kill switch |
| OWASP LLM #1? | Prompt injection |
| Shadow AI fix? | Approved tools, acceptable-use policy, DLP/blocks, training, easy intake |
| Board KRIs? | Inventory coverage, high-risk assessments done, incidents, fairness/drift breaches, overdue reviews, shadow-AI detections |
