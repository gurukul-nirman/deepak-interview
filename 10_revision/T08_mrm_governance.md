# T08 · MRM governance & regulation — quick refresher

## 60-second summary
- **SR 26-2 (17 Apr 2026)** replaced SR 11-7 (+ SR 21-8, OCC 2011-12, OCC 1997-24). Most relevant > **$30bn**; non-compliance ≠ supervisory criticism; **"complex"** model definition (no simple spreadsheets/deterministic rules); **inherent risk × materiality (exposure + purpose)**; **risk-based cadence** (no default annual); **GenAI/agentic AI out of scope**; vendor section; AI RFI announced.
- **PRA SS1/23** (effective 17 May 2024): 5 principles — identification & classification · governance (SMF) · development/implementation/use · independent validation · model risk mitigants (PMAs).
- **ECB guide** revised 28 Jul 2025 (ML expectations) · **OSFI E-23** effective 1 May 2027 (AI/ML in scope) · **RBI draft MRM** 24 Jun 2026 (all REs, AI/ML).

## Quick Q → short answer
| Q | A |
|---|---|
| Model risk? | Adverse consequences from incorrect or misused model outputs |
| SR 11-7 validation elements? | Conceptual soundness · ongoing monitoring (process verification, benchmarking) · outcomes analysis (back-testing) |
| Effective challenge: SR 11-7 vs SR 26-2? | Incentives, competence, influence → expertise, independence, organisational standing & influence |
| Is a spreadsheet a model? | Simple arithmetic no (SR 26-2); one implementing a statistical model yes; PRA wants proportionate controls on simple tools |
| 3LoD? | Owners/developers/users · MRM/validation · internal audit |
| Tiering? | Inherent risk × materiality → depth and cadence |
| Material change? | Methodology, variables, segmentation, data source, use, or output impact above threshold |
| MRM KRIs? | Overdue validations, open/overdue High findings, restricted models, monitoring breaches, inventory completeness, overlay size |
| Does SR 26-2 mean less rigour at a global bank? | No — PRA/ECB/OSFI still apply; use it to re-tier and focus |
| Internal audit's role? | Assess MRM effectiveness, not re-validate |
