## 60-second summary
- EL = PD × LGD × EAD (provisions/pricing); UL → capital.
- EAD(revolving) = Drawn + CCF × (Limit − Drawn). LGD = 1 − recovery; retail with cures: (1 − cure rate) × LGD_non-cure.
- US charge-off: cards **180 DPD**, instalment **120 DPD**. Basel default: 90 DPD or unlikely to pay.
