# Applications tracker — the pipeline that produces interviews

> Prep without a pipeline produces no offers. Targets come from `00_strategy/01_market_reality_and_targets.md` §3 and the
> job-search track in `00_strategy/03_six_week_plan.md`. Tell Claude in plain English ("applied to Citi MRM AVP via
> referral from …", "HSBC recruiter called, band 32–36") and it updates this file; `STATUS` summarises it.

**Funnel check (Sundays):** applications → recruiter screens → technical rounds → offers. If fewer than 1 in 10
applications gets a screen after three weeks, the problem is the résumé or the targeting, not interview skill — fix those
first (`REVIEW RESUME`, referrals over portals). **[Assumption — typical funnel; calibrate on your own numbers]**
