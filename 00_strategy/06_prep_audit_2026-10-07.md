> **What this is:** a full review of this repo — every study file, the plan, the mock system, both projects and all the
> code (executed, not just read) — from the seat of a VP / Head of Model Risk hiring for a credit-model validation role
> at an Indian bank GCC. Each area is rated out of 10, gaps and errors are listed, and the fixes already applied are
> recorded in §6. Labels: **[Certain]** verified · **[Likely]** well supported · **[Assumption]** judgment.

## 0. Verdict in six lines
1. **The library and the simulator are excellent — 8.5/10.** Accurate, current, VP-bar material, and a mock-interview
   system most candidates will never have. More content is not what stands between you and the offer.
2. **Readiness today is 1/10, which is normal for day 3 — but the plan was aimed at the wrong 20%.** It spent most hours
   on knowledge breadth and very few on what decides offers for your profile: your own evidence, level signals, résumé
   and pipeline, and spoken delivery.
3. **The code would have failed on your Windows laptop on day 1.** The coding grader, the P1 pipeline and two demos
   crashed. All fixed and re-verified (§4).
4. **One content error and three currency gaps** that an interviewer would catch were fixed: the IFRS 9 scenario example,
   the EU AI Omnibus (now law), RBI ECL floors, and the US Basel endgame re-proposal that would delete the advanced
   approaches your IRB models probably sit under.
5. **The projects are good.** P1 is the strongest single asset in the repo; P2 is well built but low-ROI for India credit
   roles, so it's now a compressed 3-hour memo.
6. **On your expectation:** ₹40L fixed is reachable but it is the **stretch**, not the base case. Plan for it, but decide
   your acceptable floor in writing now (§1.3).
