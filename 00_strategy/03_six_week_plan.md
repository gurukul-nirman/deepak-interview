## Rules that make 85 hours enough
1. **Output every session.** Each block ends with something written or spoken: an answer, code, a finding, a story.
   Reading without output doesn't count.
2. **The diagnostic decides.** Skim topics marked 🟢; spend saved time on 🔴/🟠. `STATUS` shows what's weakest.
3. **Exam mode is uncomfortable — that's the point.** No hints during mocks. Feedback comes in the record.
4. **Speak, don't type.** Use dictation for mock answers and paste them unedited (protocol §2 rule 7).
5. **Close the loop the same week.** Study every gap a mock lists; run `REVISE` a few days later.
6. **Slip rule.** If you miss a session, don't push the whole plan back. Drop the lowest-priority item that week, in this
   order: P2 → P2/P3 topics (T14–T18) → study of 🟢 topics → the full-process simulation. **Never drop** topic mocks
   (`T12` above all), your evidence work, or applications.
7. **Real-interview rule.** When a real interview is scheduled, the next 1–2 sessions become: JD prep
   (`07_jd_analysis/`) → `START PROCESS JD: <paste>` for the round you'll face → `REVISE` the JD's main topics. Within
   24 hours afterwards: `DEBRIEF <company> <round>`.
8. **End every session** with "commit and push". On Sundays, ask for a PR to `main` and **merge it**.
